What if CT is closed or it's lunch?

Florida registered-office hours under Fla. Stat. 48.091 are at least 10 a.m.–noon and 2–4 p.m., excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays. We plan the service window around those hours and confirm it in our reply.
